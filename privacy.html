Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Afterimage is a photo frame for iPad and Mac. It is designed so that your photos stay on your device. This policy explains what the app handles and why.

Your photos stay on your device

  • Afterimage reads the photos and albums you give it access to through Apple's Photos framework. You can give access to all your photos or only to the ones you select, and change it at any time in your device's Settings.
  • Photos are resized and shown on your device. Afterimage never uploads, copies to a server or shares your photos, album names, the people in them, their locations, their dates or any other photo metadata.
  • To frame each photo well, Afterimage uses Apple's Vision framework on your device to find faces and the photo's main subject. Only the positions of those areas are kept, in a cache on your device. Afterimage does not recognize or identify people.
  • Afterimage never changes, deletes or adds photos in your library.

What Afterimage does not collect

  • Afterimage has no user accounts and runs no servers of its own.
  • Afterimage does not show ads, does not use your device's advertising identifier, does not track you across other apps or websites, and does not sell personal data.

Anonymous usage statistics

To learn which features are used and to fix problems, Afterimage uses Google Analytics for Firebase. It records product events such as “photo access was granted”, “an album was chosen”, “the frame started” or “a setting was changed”, with coarse values like the style in use or whether the clock is on. Events never include album names, photo details, people, places or dates.

  • Firebase assigns a random app-instance identifier and collects standard technical data: device model, operating system and app version, language, approximate country derived from the IP address, and session timing.
  • These statistics are not linked to your identity and are not used for advertising.
  • You can turn this off at any time in Afterimage › Settings › Privacy › Share anonymous usage data. Turning it off stops collection.
  • Google processes this data on our behalf under the Firebase privacy terms.

Crash reports

When Afterimage crashes or hits an unexpected error, Firebase Crashlytics records a technical report: the code location, device model, operating system and app version. Error messages are cleaned of identifiers, file paths, web addresses and e-mail addresses before they are sent. Crash reports follow the same Share anonymous usage data setting and are never used for advertising.

Afterimage Pro purchases

Afterimage Pro is bought through the App Store; Apple handles the payment and Afterimage never sees your payment details. To know whether Pro is active on your devices, Afterimage uses RevenueCat, which receives a random, anonymous app user ID together with the purchase information from Apple (product, date and renewal status). It is not linked to your name or e-mail. RevenueCat processes this data on our behalf under the RevenueCat privacy policy.

Camera and hand gestures

Hand gestures are an optional, experimental feature on iPad and Mac and are off by default. If you turn them on, Afterimage asks for permission to use the front camera and uses it only while the frame is on screen, to follow the position and shape of one hand.

  • Each camera frame is analyzed on your device with Apple's Vision framework and discarded immediately. No photo, video or image of you is ever stored, sent anywhere or shared.
  • The camera turns off when the frame closes, when Afterimage goes to the background or when you turn the feature off in Afterimage › Settings › Hand gestures. You can also withdraw camera access at any time in your iPad's Settings app or your Mac's System Settings.
  • Anonymous usage statistics only record which gesture was used (for example “next photo by gesture”), never camera data.

Data stored on your device

  • Resized copies of photos, kept in the app's cache so the frame opens quickly. The system may clear this cache at any time.
  • Framing data: the positions of faces and subjects found in each photo.
  • Your preferences, such as the chosen album, style, clock, night and privacy settings.

Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Children

Afterimage is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
