Common questions

Some photos or albums don't appear.

If you gave Afterimage access to only some photos, it can show only those. Go to Settings › Photos › Choose photos in Afterimage to add more, or allow all photos in your iPad's Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos › Afterimage. Screenshots are left out on purpose. Photos stored only in iCloud are downloaded the first time they're shown, so the iPad needs to be online.

How does the frame start?

Choose an album and leave the screen alone: after the time set in Settings › Ambient mode › Start after, the frame opens. Tap anywhere to go back to your albums.

How do I keep the screen on?

Turn on Settings › Display › Keep the screen on. For a frame that runs all day, keep the iPad plugged in.

Why don't I see hand gestures?

Hand gestures are an experimental Afterimage Pro feature that uses the front camera while the frame is on screen. They need an iPad with iPadOS 16 or later or a Mac with Apple silicon, so the option doesn't appear on iPads that stay on iPadOS 15.

Does Afterimage work on a Mac?

Yes, on Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or later): get it from the Mac App Store, where it appears as an iPad app. Afterimage Pro bought with the same Apple Account is shared with your Mac; if it doesn't appear, use Settings › Afterimage Pro › Restore purchases.

How do I cancel the yearly plan?

The yearly plan is managed by Apple. Open Settings › Afterimage Pro › Manage subscription in Afterimage, or your Apple Account's subscriptions in the Settings app. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.
