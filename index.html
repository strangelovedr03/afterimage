Private by design

Your photos never leave your device.

Afterimage reads your photos with Apple's Photos framework and prepares them right on the iPad or Mac. There's no account to create, no upload and no server of ours in between.

  • Any album you already have. Recents, Favorites, your own albums and iCloud shared albums, so photos a family member adds show up by themselves.
  • Only what you choose. Give Afterimage access to all your photos or just a selection; you can change it at any time.
  • Smart framing on the device. Apple's Vision framework finds faces and the subject of each photo so cropping keeps them in view. The analysis runs locally and stays there.

Styles

Three ways to show an album.

After a few minutes without a touch, Afterimage opens the frame. Any tap brings you back.

Frame

One photo at a time with a slow, gentle motion. Photos that don't fit are cropped around faces, or shown whole over a soft blur of themselves.

Drift PRO

Photos float around the clock like prints on a table. Give one a push with your finger.

Mosaic PRO

A wall of photos where one tile changes at a time, slowly reshuffling the album.

Made for a shelf

A screen that's always on, and always calm.

  • Keep the screen on. The iPad doesn't go to sleep while Afterimage is open.
  • A clock that never burns in. It moves a little every minute. Choose its style, position and brightness, or hide it.
  • On this day. Photos taken on this date in earlier years appear first, with how long ago they were taken.
  • Rest at night. Dim the screen on a schedule and slow the frame down during the night; touches still work while it's dimmed.
  • Gentle on older tablets. A lighter animation mode keeps older iPads smooth and uses less power.

Hand gestures · Experimental · Pro

Change the photo without touching the screen.

Across the room or with your hands busy? Raise an open hand and Afterimage follows it.

  1. ✋
    Wake it up

    Show an open hand for half a second.

  2. 👋
    Next or previous

    A quick swipe to the right or to the left.

  3. ✊
    Pause or resume

    Hold a closed fist for a second.
